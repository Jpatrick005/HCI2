Graphic Design
M.F.A. Program

The Yale School of Art's Graphic Design program emphasizes the development of students' personal design practices through a rigorous, deeply engaged core curriculum.

Our approach cultivates self-initiated research, group critiques of works-in-progress, and mentorship from industry practitioners. Students present their work through a culminating project and the Thesis Book.
