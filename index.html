EST. 1869 | NEW HAVEN, CONNECTICUT

A community of critical dialogue and rigorous studio practice.

The Yale School of Art opened its doors on October 15, 1869, becoming the first art school affiliated with a university of higher learning in the United States.

The school offers rigorous MFA programs in Graphic Design, Painting/Printmaking, Photography, and Sculpture, fostering a community of critical dialogue and creative exploration.
